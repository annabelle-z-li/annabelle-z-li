# Hi, I'm Annabelle 👋

I'm a middle school student researcher passionate about the intersection of
music and AI — building tools that explore what machine learning can
and *can't* yet do with sound and musical structure.

---

## What I'm Researching

Currently I'm researching this question:

"How do the architectural and computational constraints of AI music transcription models affect their ability to accurately represent the full range of musical information — including pitch, rhythm, dynamics, and instrumentation — and what are the implications for making these tools accessible and useful in real musical contexts?"

This question matters to me because when I try to transcribe the music I love, I always find it to be tedious and time-consuming. But I. and many others believe that AI could be the solution to this, like the recent research on Automatic Music Transcription (AMT). I know that there has been some tension between AI generation and music, as well as all art in general; but I believe that using AI as a tool for music can help budding musicians such as myself on our path, maybe even senior musicians. Thank you for reading :)

## Current Projects

**🎵 Music to Sheet Music**
Audio → MIDI → MusicXML → SVG sheet music pipeline using Basic Pitch (ONNX),
music21, and LilyPond. Includes an ongoing research study on the limitations
of AMT models in representing musical structure accurately.
[→ View on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/spaces/annabelle-li/music-to-sheet-music)

**🎤 NYSSMA Sight-Singing Practice**
Gradio app that generates ABC notation melodies across all six NYSSMA levels,
with microphone recording and pitch/rhythm scoring powered by librosa.
[→ View on Hugging Face](#)

**🎼 Music Composer Lab: Opera & Jazz**
Text generation app using SmolLM2-360M-Instruct with dynamic visual theming
and mode-specific prompting for opera and jazz styles.
[→ View on Hugging Face](#)
